Privacy policy

hub-backup · Last updated October 26, 2026

hub-backup is a private application used only by its owner. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the drive.file permission, hub-backup reads and writes only the backup files it created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot access any other Drive files or any other Google data.

How data is used

Backups are encrypted on the owner's own computer before upload. The data is used only to store and restore the owner's backups.

Sharing and storage

No data is collected by, sent to, or shared with anyone else. There are no analytics, ads or third-party services. The only copy is in the owner's Google Drive.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page.
